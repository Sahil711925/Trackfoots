What this page does and does not prove

Proves: the log on the operator's disk still hashes to the roots that were published, one batch at a time, and that each new checkpoint root extends the previous one (append-only).
Does not prove: that every event was logged in the first place. A log can be complete and tamper-evident, or incomplete but tamper-evident — only the first is verifiable here.
